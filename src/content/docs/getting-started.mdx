The Cobblenav mod is inspired by the item and related mechanics featured in the games Pokémon RSE and Pokémon ORAS. The mod adds a PokéNav that allows you to find out which Pokémon may appear in the area you are currently in, the chances of them appearing, and the factors that influence their appearance. The mod also allows you to track appearing Pokémon using a three-dimensional arrow or a radar item called PokéFinder. The mod is in active development, with new features added every month.
